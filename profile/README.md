### 👋 Welcome to CyBiotic Society!
We are a student-led cybersecurity association at **Högskolan i Halmstad** dedicated to bridging the gap between academic theory and real-world security practice. Whether you are taking your very first steps in Linux and networking or building advanced detection engineering pipelines, this is your space to learn, build, and collaborate.

### Explore Our Repositories
* **[The CyBiotic Wheel](https://github.com/cybiotic-society/cybiotic-wheel)** – Our comprehensive career and learning compass covering 8 cybersecurity & DFIR domains with free labs, badges, certifications, and portfolio projects.
* **[Discord Verification Bot](https://github.com/cybiotic-society/verification-bot)** – Automated domain verification bot and RBAC gateway verifying `@student.hh.se` emails via one-time codes before granting server access.
* **Workshops & Hands-On Labs** – *(Coming soon)* Exercises, starter templates, and walkthroughs from our practical workshops (Git & VS Code, SIEM/EDR, CTFs, and more).

### 🤝 How to Get Involved
1. **Explore [The CyBiotic Wheel](https://github.com/cybiotic-society/cybiotic-wheel)** and pick a track that sparks your curiosity.
2. **Contribute via Pull Requests:** Found a great free resource, tool, or CTF platform? Fork the repo, add it to the right track, and submit a PR!
3. **Join our events & workshops:** Keep an eye on our community channels for upcoming hands-on sessions.
